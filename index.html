About Us

Two designers. One shared obsession.

B&Z Visuals is a creative studio founded by Zach and Brody. We blend precision and emotion in everything we design — from brand identities and packaging to print and digital work.

The goal is work that feels effortless but is built with real intention. Every line, every kerning pair, every color choice has a reason behind it.
